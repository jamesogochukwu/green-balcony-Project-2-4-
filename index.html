Start with a plant you will use or enjoy. Basil and parsley are convenient choices for sunny spaces; leaf lettuce is useful when you have a cooler, partly sunny spot. Check the plant label for its exact light and space needs.

Yes. Most standard planters need drainage holes so extra water can escape. Choose lightweight potting mix rather than digging up heavy garden soil, and keep saucers emptied after watering.

Feel the potting mix just below the surface. Water according to the plant's needs when it begins to dry, and continue until excess water exits the drainage holes. Hot, windy balconies often dry out faster.

Many fruiting vegetables need around six or more hours of direct sun. Leafy greens often tolerate less. Observe your space over a day, then use the plant tag and local growing guidance to match plants to available light.
